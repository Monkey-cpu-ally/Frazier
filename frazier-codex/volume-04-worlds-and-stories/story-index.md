# Volume 04 — Worlds and Stories

This volume stores Frazier's story universes, characters, tones, rules, series bibles, and timelines.

## Major Story Worlds

| Codex ID | Story / World | Status | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| FC-STORY-0001 | Dark Elsewhere | Active universe | Connected horror/mystery universe with multiple series and shared mythology. |
| FC-STORY-0002 | War Dogs | Active concept | Animated with less dialogue, inspired by visual storytelling energy similar to primal/samurai-style pacing. Tone includes Bloodborne influence. |
| FC-STORY-0003 | The Sunken City / Missing of Dunwich | Active concept | Two-part story: father journalist before sinking, daughter after sinking. Townsfolk are occultists worshipping something in the sea. |
| FC-STORY-0004 | The Night Band | Children's story | Classic Disney-like fairy tale about music, fairies, a horn, and a young girl. |
| FC-STORY-0005 | Hyper Axel | Game/story project | Pixel platformer adventure about Axel, Scrap, Ronni, spirits, bosses, and upgrade systems. |
| FC-STORY-0006 | The Herald | Dark supernatural action concept | Blade-inspired horror/angel/demon mythology with estate, weapons, crests, and relics. |

## Creative Rule

Story entries should preserve:

- Original concept
- Main characters
- Tone references
- World rules
- Visual style
- Timeline
- Connected-universe links
- Future plot hooks
