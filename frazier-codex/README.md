# The Frazier Codex

The Frazier Codex is the living knowledge system for Frazier's ideas, projects, stories, designs, research notes, and ATLAS AI development.

This folder is designed to become the clean, searchable layer built on top of raw ChatGPT conversations and future project notes.

## Purpose

The Codex exists so Ajani, Minerva, Hermes, and the ATLAS Council can eventually search, understand, connect, and build from Frazier's original ideas without losing the history behind them.

## Core Rule

Raw conversations should be preserved as an archive. Structured Codex entries should summarize, organize, and connect those conversations without overwriting the original record.

## Main Volumes

- `volume-00-origin-archive/` — raw conversation summaries, source notes, and timeline references.
- `volume-01-frazier-mind/` — principles, design philosophy, invention rules, and taste profile.
- `volume-02-atlas/` — Ajani, Minerva, Hermes, Council, HUD, memory systems, AIon language, and AI architecture.
- `volume-03-engineering-projects/` — robotics, energy, manufacturing, biotech-adjacent engineering, scanners, machines, and prototypes.
- `volume-04-worlds-and-stories/` — Dark Elsewhere, Hyper Axel, The Night Band, and other creative universes.
- `volume-05-research-library/` — verified outside research, papers, documentation, references, and source summaries.
- `volume-06-design-language/` — visual style, materials, motion, interface rules, architecture, robots, vehicles, and product aesthetics.
- `volume-07-frazier-timeline/` — chronological evolution of ideas and project versions.
- `templates/` — repeatable templates for new entries.

## Lead AI Roles

- **Ajani** — strategy, leadership, business, security, planning, and execution.
- **Minerva** — science, biology, nature, education, medicine-adjacent research, and story/world depth.
- **Hermes** — engineering, robotics, manufacturing, architecture, software systems, and optimization.
- **Council** — cross-checks major decisions and connects ideas across domains.
